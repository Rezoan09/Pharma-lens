How it works

Three steps. No analyst queue.

Researchers, medical affairs and commercial teams get answers from their own data without waiting on a statistician or building formulas by hand.

STEP 01
📂

Bring your dataset

Drop in an Excel or CSV file: trial results, patient outcomes, survey or sales data. Columns are detected and typed automatically.

STEP 02
🧮

Statistics are computed

Mean, median, spread, quartiles and outliers are calculated in code for every numeric variable, and category breakdowns are built for the rest.

STEP 03
📊

Read, explore, export

Review the dashboard, charts and distributions, read the AI-written findings, then export one multi-section PDF report.

Scientific research capability

The numbers come from code. The language comes from AI.

A general chatbot reads your data and estimates. PharmaLens separates the two jobs, so every figure is reproducible and the AI only explains it.

📐

Descriptive statistics

Mean, median, minimum, maximum, standard deviation and quartiles for every numeric variable, calculated deterministically.

🎯

Outlier detection

Values are flagged with the standard Tukey IQR fences, a method researchers already recognize and can cite in a methods section.

📈

Distribution analysis

Histograms and category breakdowns show skew, spread and imbalance that averages alone would hide.
